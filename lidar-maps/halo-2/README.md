# Halo 2 LiDAR maps

## Author Provided Scans

The mod author's own scans of 25 Halo 2 maps in one zip: [lidar-maps-halo-2.zip](https://github.com/AyoItzKye/project-proving-grounds/releases/download/lidar-maps/lidar-maps-halo-2.zip) (0.7 MB, 2026-11-12).

Easiest: in the launcher, open **LiDAR scans**, choose Halo 2 and press **Import**. Or download the zip and choose it with **Import a shared zip ...** there.

Maps in it: h2_ascension, h2_backwash, h2_beavercreek, h2_burial_mounds, h2_coagulation, h2_colossus, h2_containment, h2_cyclotron, h2_deltatap, h2_derelict, h2_dune, h2_elongation, h2_foundation, h2_gemini, h2_headlong, h2_highplains, h2_lockout, h2_midship, h2_needle, h2_street_sweeper, h2_triplicate, h2_turf, h2_warlock, h2_waterworks, h2_zanzibar.

## Player scans

Shared LiDAR scans of Halo 2 maps, one zip per scan, named `<map>_<date>.zip` (the map's folder name, such as h2_lockout, h2_ascension).

To use one: download it and import it (see [the LiDAR maps page](../README.md)). It adds to your own scan of that map; it never replaces it.

No scans shared yet. Be the first: [share a LiDAR map](https://github.com/AyoItzKye/project-proving-grounds/issues/new?template=lidar_map.yml).
