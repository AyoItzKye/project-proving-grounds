# Halo 2 Anniversary LiDAR maps

## Author Provided Scans

The mod author's own scans of 7 Halo 2 Anniversary maps in one zip: [lidar-maps-halo-2-anniversary.zip](https://github.com/AyoItzKye/project-proving-grounds/releases/download/lidar-maps/lidar-maps-halo-2-anniversary.zip) (0.7 MB, 2026-11-12).

Easiest: in the launcher, open **LiDAR scans**, choose Halo 2 Anniversary and press **Import**. Or download the zip and choose it with **Import a shared zip ...** there.

Maps in it: h2a_ca_ascension, h2a_ca_coagulation, h2a_ca_lockout, h2a_ca_relic, h2a_ca_sanctuary, h2a_ca_warlock, h2a_ca_zanzibar.

## Player scans

Shared LiDAR scans of Halo 2 Anniversary maps, one zip per scan, named `<map>_<date>.zip` (the map's folder name, such as h2a_ca_lockout, h2a_ca_ascension).

To use one: download it and import it (see [the LiDAR maps page](../README.md)). It adds to your own scan of that map; it never replaces it.

No scans shared yet. Be the first: [share a LiDAR map](https://github.com/AyoItzKye/project-proving-grounds/issues/new?template=lidar_map.yml).
