# Halo 4 LiDAR maps

## Author Provided Scans

The mod author's own scans of 21 Halo 4 maps in one zip: [lidar-maps-halo-4.zip](https://github.com/AyoItzKye/project-proving-grounds/releases/download/lidar-maps/lidar-maps-halo-4.zip) (0.6 MB, 2026-11-12).

Easiest: in the launcher, open **LiDAR scans**, choose Halo 4 and press **Import**. Or download the zip and choose it with **Import a shared zip ...** there.

Maps in it: h4_ca_basin, h4_ca_blood_cavern, h4_ca_blood_crash, h4_ca_canyon, h4_ca_creeper, h4_ca_deadlycrossing, h4_ca_dropoff, h4_ca_gore_valley, h4_ca_highrise, h4_ca_port, h4_ca_rattler, h4_ca_redoubt, h4_ca_spiderweb, h4_ca_tower, h4_ca_warhouse, h4_dlc_dejewel, h4_dlc_dejunkyard, h4_wraparound, h4_z05_cliffside, h4_z11_valhalla, h4_zd_02_grind.

## Player scans

Shared LiDAR scans of Halo 4 maps, one zip per scan, named `<map>_<date>.zip` (the map's folder name, such as h4_ca_warhouse, h4_ca_canyon).

To use one: download it and import it (see [the LiDAR maps page](../README.md)). It adds to your own scan of that map; it never replaces it.

No scans shared yet. Be the first: [share a LiDAR map](https://github.com/AyoItzKye/project-proving-grounds/issues/new?template=lidar_map.yml).
