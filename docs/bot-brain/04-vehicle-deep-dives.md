# Vehicle deep dives

How to drive, gun, ride and kill every vehicle, with the specific tricks per game.

## Warthog family

### Driving

- Lines: drive in arcs that keep the gunner's side facing the enemy and the engine block between the driver and the main threat. Never drive straight at a group; drive past it at 30 to 40 ft so the gunner gets a full pass.
- E-brake (CE, H2, H3): tap the brake while turning to slide the rear and reverse direction in a small space; use it to turn around at the end of a pass or to dodge a rocket. Reach and H4 have a weaker slide.
- Speed and terrain: the hog flips on steep rocks and loses the gunner's aim on bumps; keep to roads and gentle slopes while the gunner is firing, cut across rough ground only to escape.
- Reversing: the hog reverses almost as fast as it drives forward; reversing out of a fight keeps the gunner on target and the engine block toward the enemy.
- Splatter: a hog at speed kills anything it touches; aim for enemies on flat ground who are strafing, not for enemies on ledges. In H2 onward a boarded hog is a lost hog, so splatter attempts on an enemy who is standing still near cover are risky.
- Flipping: a flipped hog is righted by holding the use key; the driver should bail and flip only when no enemy is within Medium band.
- Flag taxi: the runner rides the passenger seat (the only seat that can hold the flag); the driver circles back to the flag, stops for one second, and drives the covered route home. The gunner stays mounted.
- Repair: no repair in Halo; a smoking hog is abandoned near friendly territory so it respawns fresh.

### Gunning

- Chain gun: spin-up delay of about 0.5 s (longer in Reach); start firing a half second before the target is in the reticle. Overheats in Reach after about 6 s of continuous fire; fire in 3 s bursts.
- Lead: the chain gun is projectile; lead strafing targets by a quarter body at Medium band, more at Long band.
- Priority: the player with anti-vehicle weapons (laser, rockets, plasma pistol glow, plasma launcher) first; then whoever is shooting the driver; then everyone else.
- Gauss cannon: one shot kills players and Ghosts, two for a Warthog, several for a tank. Aim for the vehicle's body; it is hitscan. Rate of fire is slow, so make each shot count.
- Rocket hog: a volley of rockets that tracks; fire at vehicles and groups.
- The gunner is exposed: a bot gunner with its shield popped tells the driver to retreat (the scripting agent should let the driver read the gunner's shield).

### Passenger

- Shoot with a precision weapon at anyone the gunner cannot reach (behind the hog), throw plasma grenades at vehicles the hog passes, and carry the flag.
- The passenger can be a rocket user on big maps: the hog drives the rocket user into range of the enemy tank.

### Anti-hog play on foot

- Hide behind hard cover until the hog commits to a pass, then shoot the gunner from behind the hog as it passes (the gunner cannot turn fast enough).
- Stick the hog as it passes; aim at the gunner's body. One stick kills the hog in H2 and H3; two in Reach.
- Plasma pistol EMP, then board: boarding a hog throws the driver out in H2 onward (the boarder takes the driver seat); the gunner can then be meleed from inside.
- Rockets: aim at the front wheels when it drives toward the bot; the hog flips and the crew is thrown.
- Laser: wait for a level drive-by; the beam kills the hog and the crew.
- Trip mine (H3) on the hog's road; sticky detonator (H4) on the lane.
- Grenade at the ground under a stopped hog flips it.

### Per-game notes

- CE: no boarding; the hog is killed by rockets, grenades or by shooting the driver through the windscreen with the pistol (the driver has no protection). Two hogs per base on Blood Gulch and Sidewinder; the gunner is the main damage source in BTB.
- H2: boarding introduced; the hog is fragile; the Gauss hog appears on Coagulation and Containment and is the strongest vehicle on those maps.
- H3: the hog is tougher; the chopper rams kill it in one hit; the laser is its main predator; the hog on Valhalla, Standoff and Sandtrap decides the match.
- Reach: the hog is tough and the turret spins up slowly; the plasma launcher is its main predator; armor lock on a boarder's approach saves the hog.
- H4: the hog is tough; the Mantis and the railgun are its predators; the gunner's turret has no overheat.

## Ghost, Mongoose, Chopper, Revenant, Spectre, Prowler

### Ghost

- Strafing: the Ghost strafes sideways at full speed; fire the plasma cannons while strafing and never sit still. Two cannons kill a full-shield player in about 1.5 s at Short to Medium band.
- Boost splatter: boost (H2 onward) for a straight-line charge that kills on contact; use it on enemies in the open who are running in a straight line, on flag carriers, and on enemies climbing a slope. Boost is a commitment: the Ghost cannot turn while boosting, so a sidestepping enemy gets a free melee or stick.
- Approach angle: come at an enemy from the side or behind; from the front a precision weapon kills the exposed driver.
- Against vehicles: a Ghost loses to a Warthog gunner head-on; it wins by circling behind the hog and shooting the gunner, or by boosting past and leaving.
- Exiting: the driver is exposed on exit; exit behind cover only.
- Counter on foot: sidestep the boost at the last moment, melee the side (two melees kill a Ghost in H2 and H3), stick it, EMP and board. Never run straight away from a boosting Ghost.
- CE: no boost; the Ghost in CE is a fast gun platform that dies to pistol fire on the driver.

### Mongoose

- Transport and flag runner: the passenger can shoot and carry the flag; the driver cannot. Drive the covered route and never stop.
- No armor: one grenade or a few BR bursts kill the crew; the Mongoose is for getting somewhere, not for fighting.
- Splatter works at full speed on flat ground; a Mongoose into a crouched sniper is a kill.
- The Mongoose flips easily; drive straight over bumps and jumps, not sideways.
- Counter: shoot the driver; one grenade; a bigger vehicle's splatter.

### Chopper (H3)

- Ram: boost into any vehicle and it is destroyed; boost into a player and they are splattered. The ram is the Chopper's main weapon on Sandtrap and Avalanche.
- Cannons: strong against infantry at Medium band; fire while circling.
- The driver is exposed from the sides and behind; the Chopper turns slowly, so flankers kill it.
- Counter: sidestep the ram, shoot the driver, stick it.

### Revenant (Reach)

- A two-seat plasma mortar buggy: fast, strong splash from the mortar, a passenger seat for a shooter or flag runner. Fire the mortar at groups and at the Warthog's gunner.
- Fragile; the crew is exposed; the plasma launcher and the laser kill it in one shot.

### Spectre (H2)

- The Covenant transport: driver, gunner, two passengers; the gunner's plasma turret is weaker than the hog's chain gun but the Spectre boosts. Use it as a flag taxi and a hog hunter from behind.

### Prowler (H3)

- Driver, gunner, two side passengers; fast; the gunner's turret is strong at Medium band; the crew is exposed from all sides. Use it as a four-person flag taxi on Sandtrap and keep it moving.

## Scorpion, Wraith, Mantis

### Scorpion

- Cannon: one shot kills any player and any light vehicle; two to three for another tank. Reload is about 2.5 s. The shell is a fast projectile; lead moving vehicles by a vehicle length at Long band.
- Machine gun: the coaxial gun (and the H3 passenger seats) handles infantry at Short band; the bot switches to it when an enemy is within 30 ft.
- Blind spots: directly in front below the barrel's depression, and directly behind. Enemies there can only be killed by the escort or by driving away.
- Position: stay behind the infantry line at Medium to Long band from the enemy; fire at where enemies cluster (doorways, cover points, the flag stand). Never drive into an enclosed area or a tunnel.
- Escort: one bot within Short band of the tank at all times with a precision weapon, watching for boarders and plasma pistols. Without an escort the tank retreats to base.
- Threats: laser (one shot), rockets (two to three), plasma stickies (two), boarding (one grenade), another tank. The tank turns behind cover when a laser charge is heard.
- Passengers (H3, H4): the four side seats let a crew ride and shoot; use them as a mobile firebase on Sandtrap and Exile.
- CE: no boarding; the tank is only killed by rockets, stickies, or another tank; the driver is exposed from above and can be sniped.

### Wraith

- Mortar: a slow arcing plasma shell with a large splash; aim at the ground where enemies will be in about 1.5 s (the travel time at Medium band). Strong against groups, vehicles and anyone holding a fixed position.
- Boost: the Wraith boosts for a splatter and to escape; boost into a Warthog to destroy it.
- Gunner (H3, Reach, H4): the plasma turret handles close infantry; without a gunner the Wraith is helpless at Short band.
- Blind spot: everything within about 30 ft; the mortar cannot hit close targets.
- Counter on foot: move while the shell is in the air, close in through cover, board and plant a grenade (two plants kill), or stick it. Strafe sideways, never straight toward or away.

### Mantis (H4)

- Walker with a machine gun and a missile pod; a shield that recharges; a stomp that kills anyone within about 8 ft; it can crouch for stability.
- Use: hold a choke or an objective at Medium band; fire missiles at vehicles and the machine gun at infantry; stomp anyone who closes. Stay near cover to recharge the shield.
- Weaknesses: slow turning, a plasma pistol or pulse grenade EMP stalls it, railgun and laser kill it quickly, boarding (after the EMP or from behind) plants a grenade that kills it in two.
- Counter on foot: EMP, then board from behind; or keep 15 ft away and shoot the cockpit with precision fire while it reloads; never stand in front within stomp range.

## Banshee, Hornet, Falcon

### Banshee

- Weapons: twin plasma cannons (hold the trigger, strong against infantry and light vehicles) and the fuel rod bomb (one shot kills a player, damages vehicles; slow projectile).
- Flight: strafing runs, never hovering. Fly low behind terrain between passes. Barrel roll (H2 onward) and loop to dodge rockets and lasers; roll the moment the lock tone sounds or the laser glow is seen.
- Targets: the enemy Warthog's gunner, the enemy Banshee, the flag carrier, snipers on rooftops (bomb them from above).
- Boarding: in H3 onward a player can board a Banshee from a ledge or by jumping onto it when it flies low; a bot in a Banshee never hovers near ledges.
- Counter on foot: rockets (lock on in H2, Reach and H4), laser, missile pod, plasma launcher; precision fire on the pilot when it hovers; a plasma pistol EMP drops it from the sky in H3 onward.
- CE (PC maps): no barrel roll; the Banshee dies to pistol fire on the pilot and to rockets.

### Hornet (H3)

- Pilot's missiles and machine gun plus two side passengers who can shoot and carry the flag. Hover is possible but exposes the passengers; fly low and fast.
- Strong against the Warthog and against snipers; fragile against the laser and missile pod.

### Falcon (Reach)

- A transport with two side turrets (gunners) and a pilot; the pilot has no weapon in most variants. Fly the gunners around the enemy's flank at Medium band and keep moving; land behind cover to swap crew.
- Fragile to the laser, plasma launcher and rockets; the gunners are exposed to precision fire.

## Boarding and hijacking in depth

| Vehicle | Board from | What happens | Time to complete (approx.) | Best setup |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Warthog (driver seat) | Either side, rear | Driver is thrown out; boarder takes the wheel | 1.5 s | EMP, grenade under it, or a stopped hog |
| Warthog (gunner) | Rear | Gunner is thrown out | 1.5 s | Same |
| Mongoose | Side | Driver thrown out | 1 s | Any stop |
| Ghost | Side or rear | Driver thrown out; boarder takes it | 1.5 s | Sidestep the boost, then board |
| Chopper | Side | Driver thrown out | 1.5 s | Same as Ghost |
| Scorpion | Rear or side | Boarder plants a grenade (two kill it) or melees the driver | 2 s per plant | Approach from behind; EMP does not stall a tank |
| Wraith | Rear | Grenade plant or driver melee | 2 s | While the mortar is in the air |
| Banshee | From a ledge, or when it flies low | Pilot thrown out (H3 onward) | 1.5 s | Ledges on Valhalla and Ragnarok |
| Hornet, Falcon | From a ledge | Pilot thrown out | 1.5 s | Same |
| Mantis | Rear after EMP, or any side when stalled | Grenade plant (two kill it) | 2 s | Plasma pistol or pulse grenade first |

### Rules for the boarder

- The board prompt appears at Melee band; the bot must be within arm's reach and facing the vehicle. Approach from the side the driver cannot shoot (the Ghost's side, the hog's rear).
- EMP windows: a charged plasma pistol stalls ground vehicles for about 3 s in H3 onward (Reach's grenade launcher EMP and H4's pulse grenade do the same). The bot boards within that window.
- After boarding a Ghost or hog, the bot is now the driver and the thrown driver is at Melee band behind it: drive away or splatter them.
- Boarding a tank: two grenade plants kill it (one in Reach if damaged); after the first plant the driver often bails, so the bot melees the exiting driver.
- Boarding is interrupted by damage in Reach and H4 if the bot's shield pops; board only with a full shield.

### Rules for the driver being boarded

- Prevention: never stop within 10 ft of an enemy on foot; keep speed above walking pace; splatter anyone who approaches from the front.
- When EMP'd: bail immediately if an enemy is within Short band; fight on foot and re-enter after.
- Armor lock (Reach) during a boarding attempt cancels the board and EMPs the boarder.
- A tank driver who feels a plant should drive away (the second plant is the kill) and let the escort kill the boarder.

### Per-game boarding notes

- CE: no boarding; vehicles are destroyed or their drivers shot.
- H2: boarding introduced; fast and generous; a Ghost at speed can still be boarded if it passes within reach.
- H3: EMP from the plasma pistol makes boarding reliable; Banshees and Hornets are boardable.
- Reach: boarding is slower and can be shaken; armor lock protects the driver.
- H4: boarding is fast; the Mantis is boardable after an EMP; sprint makes reaching a vehicle easier.
- H2A: Halo 4 engine boarding timings with Halo 2 vehicles.
