# Map playbooks

Per-map callouts, opening moves, team setups by mode, grenade spots and routes. Exact weapon coordinates come from the scenario tags; the playbooks say what to do with them.

## Halo: Combat Evolved playbooks

All CE weapon spots are the classic defaults and are listed by callout; the scripting agent matches them to the tag coordinates at load.

### Battle Creek (2v2 to 4v4, Slayer and CTF)

- Callouts: Red base and Blue base (each with a Roof, a Front door, a Side door and a Back ladder), Creek (the stream between), Rocks (the raised ledge with rockets), Shotgun tunnel (the cave behind the creek), Sniper ledge (above each base via ladder), the Tree.
- Opening: one bot climbs the back ladder to the sniper ledge; one throws a grenade onto Rocks then takes rockets if clear; the others hold the roof.
- Slayer setup: sniper on the roof covering the creek and the far roof; a pistol at the side door covering the shotgun tunnel exit; a pistol inside the base covering the ladders. The fourth roams the creek edge for grenades and shotgun.
- CTF: the runner goes through the shotgun tunnel; the roof sniper covers the tunnel exit; the rocket bot sits on Rocks to shoot anyone chasing. Defend from the roof and inside the base; the flag is inside, so one bot stays in the base with the shotgun.
- Grenade spots: onto the enemy roof from the creek; into the base front door from the creek bank; onto Rocks from either base roof.
- Jumps: crouch-jump from the base side ledge onto the roof without the ladder; grenade-jump to the sniper ledge.
- Traps: the enemy sniper ledge is reachable by the ladder behind their base; a bot climbing it is dead if the roof is held.

### Chill Out (2v2 and 4v4 Slayer)

- Callouts: Rocket ledge (top level above the center, reached by the teleporter), Camo (center floor), Shotgun hall, Overshield room (lower), Teleporter in and out, the Bridge.
- Opening: rockets via the teleporter are the first target; the teleporter exit is pre-nadable, so a Veteran bot throws a grenade through first.
- Setup: hold the rocket ledge and the bridge; one bot covers the teleporter exit from the ledge, one holds the shotgun at the hall corner. Camo is taken on its timer by the shotgun bot.
- Grenade spots: through the teleporter; down into the camo pit from the ledge; into the overshield room door.
- Health packs sit in the lower rooms; denying them starves a wounded enemy.

### Damnation (4v4 Slayer and CTF)

- Callouts: Top (the upper platform with overshield), Rocket room (bottom by the water), Lifts (two), Pipes, the Waterfall, Green room, the Bridge.
- Setup: hold Top with two pistols covering both lift exits; one bot takes rockets and returns to Top; one holds the Bridge.
- Rocket room has two entrances (water side and ramp); a bot inside keeps a pistol on the ramp and a grenade for the water side.
- Grenade jumps: from the Bridge to Top saves the lift ride.
- CTF: flags are at opposite ends; the lift is the fast route and the water the safe route; runners use the water with the roof sniper covering.

### Prisoner (2v2 and 4v4 Slayer)

- Callouts: Top (the upper ring), Rockets (top-mid ledge), Camo (bottom level), Overshield (top), Ladders (several), the Pillar, Bottom.
- Setup: everything is about Top; three bots on the ring at different corners, one who drops to Camo on its timer and climbs back. Anyone on Bottom is in a pit shot from four angles.
- Ladder rule: never climb a ladder with an enemy on Top; grenade the ladder top first.
- Grenade spots: down the ladders; onto the rocket ledge from across the ring.

### Hang 'Em High (4v4 and 8v8 Slayer and CTF)

- Callouts: Tombstones (the pillars field), Blue base and Red base (flag rooms), the Walkways (elevated), Tower (overshield), Pit (the trench), Rockets (in the trench mid), Snipe (each team's walkway sniper).
- Setup: snipers on the walkways cover the tombstones; the pit is avoided; rockets are rushed at the start from the base side with a grenade thrown onto the spawn.
- CTF: the flag rooms are deep in each base; the runner uses the trench side wall (out of walkway sightlines) and pops out by the base; defenders sit on the walkway above the flag room.
- Grenade spots: into the tombstone cover from the walkways; into the base flag room from the walkway above.

### Blood Gulch (8v8, CTF and Slayer)

- Callouts: Red base and Blue base (roof, inside, back), the Caves (each side, with overshield or camo), Mid rocks, the Cliffs, the Teleporter (base roof to the far side).
- Setup: two snipers (one per base roof); one Warthog crew of two with a gunner; two in the caves with rockets or plasma; the rest roam between base and mid rocks. Never cross mid on foot without the hog drawing fire.
- CTF: the hog carries the flag runner (passenger seat); the runner grabs and the hog drives around the cliff edge, not through mid. The teleporter is a fast flag escape from the enemy roof.
- Anti-hog: a plasma grenade from the cave or rocks as it passes; a pistol on the gunner from the base roof.

### Rat Race (2v2 and 4v4 Slayer)

- Callouts: Ring (the center loop), Rockets (bottom), Shotgun hall, the Ramps, the Lifts.
- Setup: hold the ring and shoot down into both halls; the shotgun bot holds the ramp corner.

### Wizard (2v2 and 4v4 Slayer)

- Callouts: Center (raised platform, rockets), four Corners (ramps), the Pits.
- Setup: hold two adjacent corners and the ramp between them; the center is taken only for the rockets and left at once. Grenades land on the center from any corner.

### Sidewinder (8v8 CTF)

- Callouts: Red and Blue base (rooftops and interiors), Tunnel (the long underpass with the overshield), Mid rocks, the Warthog ramps.
- Setup: snipers on rooftops; the flag runner uses the tunnel; hogs run the outer road with gunners.

## Halo 2 playbooks

Callouts are the competitive community names used in MLG-era Halo 2 and still used in MCC. Setups assume 4v4 BR starts; adjust for SMG starts by making the first move a run to a BR spawn.

### Lockout (Slayer, Oddball, KOTH)

- Callouts: Sniper tower (S1 bottom, S2 the middle landing with the sniper, S3 top), BR tower (BR1 bottom, BR2 middle, BR3 top), Library (the long room with the sword), Lift (the man cannon lift to BR3), Elbow (the bend between Library and Sniper tower), Bottom mid (the shotgun), Glass (the walkway with glass panels), Ramp (from Bottom mid to S2), Back (the ledge behind Sniper tower), Shotty hall.
- Opening (Slayer): two bots go to S2 for the sniper and hold S2 and S3; one goes to BR2; one goes toward Library for the sword, then back to BR tower. Nobody goes to Bottom mid at the start.
- Standard hold: S2, S3, BR2 and BR3. From these the team sees Library doors, Elbow, Glass, the lift top and Bottom mid. The enemy spawns at Bottom mid, Elbow or the Back and must climb into the team's fire.
- Grenade spots: from S2 onto Bottom mid; from BR3 onto the lift landing; from Elbow into Library's near door; onto Glass from S3.
- Sword play: the sword holder sits at the Library's inner door or at Elbow and lunges at anyone who enters; a sword flying bot can go from Library to S2 in one lunge on an enemy at S2.
- Oddball: hold the ball at the Back (behind Sniper tower) or in Library's corner; teammates on S2, S3 and Elbow. Never hold the ball at Bottom mid.
- KOTH: hills rotate between Library, Sniper tower and BR tower; the team holds the structure containing the hill and the adjacent structure.
- Jumps: BR2 to the Glass walkway; the Ramp edge to S2 without the ramp; Elbow to Library roof is a super bounce and out of bounds in competitive settings.

### Midship (Slayer, CTF, Oddball)

- Callouts: Top mid (the center ring with the sword), Bottom mid (under it), Pink 1 to Pink 3 (the pink side towers, bottom to top), Carbine (the opposite side, named for its weapon), Red and Blue base (the two bases with the flags), Ramps (from each base to Top mid), the Lifts (to Pink 3 and the carbine side top), Plasma hall.
- Opening: two bots push Top mid for the sword with grenades thrown over the center first; one goes to Pink 3 for the overlook; one holds the home ramp.
- Standard hold: Top mid plus Pink 3 (or the carbine-side top). From there the team sees both bases and both ramps. The enemy must push through the bottom or the lifts.
- Grenade spots: over Top mid from either base ramp (the classic Midship nade); onto Pink 3 from the opposite top; into Bottom mid from Top mid.
- CTF: the flag is at the base floor; the runner takes the flag and goes under Top mid (Bottom mid) to the home ramp while escorts hold Top mid and shoot down. Juggle the flag along the bottom.
- Oddball: hold at the base corner under the ramp or at Pink 1's corner; teammates on Top mid and Pink 3.
- Sword: the sword holder waits at the top of the lifts and lunges at anyone riding up; lifts are the best sword lanes in the game.

### Beaver Creek (Slayer, CTF, Neutral bomb)

- Callouts: Red roof and Blue roof (glass roofs, breakable), Red base and Blue base (interior, flag inside), Creek, Rocks (the rock walkway with rockets, above the creek), Sniper path (the cliff path on the Blue side), Magnum path (the Red side equivalent), Tunnel (the cave behind the creek), Tree.
- Opening: one bot runs the sniper path for the sniper; one goes up Rocks with a grenade thrown first for rockets; two hold the home roof and base. Both roofs see Rocks, so the rocket run is a race the sniper decides.
- Standard hold: roof with the sniper, one inside the base at the side door, rockets on Rocks or on the roof. The glass roof breaks under grenades and gunfire; a bot on a roof that hears a grenade drops inside.
- Grenade spots: onto the enemy roof from the creek bank and from Rocks; through the base front door from the creek; onto Rocks from both roofs.
- CTF: the runner uses the tunnel or the creek wall closest to the enemy sniper's blind side; the rocket bot sits on Rocks and shoots chasers; the sniper holds the home roof; one escort runs with the carrier. Halopedia's note: have the rocket holder cover or distract while the runner grabs.
- Neutral bomb: the bomb spawns at the creek; the whole team pushes to it and escorts the carrier up the enemy's side ramp to the arm point on their base.
- Jumps: crouch-jump from the side ledge onto the roof; from Rocks drop to the creek to escape.

### Sanctuary (CTF, Slayer)

- Callouts: Red and Blue base (with Flag, the Ramp, the Arch), Ring 1 (nearest each base), Ring 2, Ring 3 (the center ring), Rocks (the raised rocks on one side), Snipe (each team's sniper ledge), Carbine (the far side structure), the Water, Shotgun (the lower center).
- Opening: sniper to the home Snipe; two to Ring 1 and Rocks; one toward Ring 2 for rockets with a grenade thrown onto the spawn first.
- Standard hold: Snipe, Ring 1, Rocks. The team sees Ring 2, Ring 3 and the enemy's Ring 1. Rockets are contested on their timer from Ring 1 with two bots.
- CTF: the flag sits at the base top; the runner pulls through the back of the base, drops to the Water and runs the far side under the ring; escorts hold Ring 1 and Rocks. Defense: sniper at Snipe covering the enemy's pull lane, a BR at the Arch, a shotgun inside the base.
- Grenade spots: onto Ring 2 from Ring 1; into the base flag room from the Arch; onto Rocks from Ring 2.

### Warlock (Slayer, Oddball)

- Callouts: Center (the top platform), four Corners (named by color or by weapon), the Ramps, the Pits (the lower ring).
- Setup: hold two adjacent corners and the ramp between; pinch the center with grenades; take the center powerup on its timer and leave.
- Oddball: hold in a corner alcove; teammates on the two adjacent ramps.

### Ascension (Slayer, CTF, KOTH)

- Callouts: Big tower (the main dish tower with the sniper on top), Small tower (the opposite tower), Bridge (between them), Dish (the satellite dish with rockets near it), Back lift (the lift to the dish side), Rocks (the upper rocks), Banshee pad, the Ring (the outer walkway).
- Opening: sniper to the Big tower top; one to the dish for rockets; one on the Banshee (if enabled) or the Back lift; one at the Bridge.
- Standard hold: Big tower top and Bridge; the sniper sees the whole map. Rockets counter the Banshee.
- CTF: flags sit at the tower bases; the runner uses the outer Ring and the Back lift; the sniper covers from the tower.
- Grenade spots: onto the Bridge from the Big tower; onto the dish from the Rocks.

### Ivory Tower (Slayer, Oddball)

- Callouts: Top walkway (rockets), Lift (bottom to top), Lift top, Balcony (sniper), Glass room, Sword room (bottom), Shotgun hall, Overshield.
- Setup: hold the top walkway and the lift top; the sword bot sits at the bottom of the lift and lunges at anyone who comes down; the balcony sniper covers the glass room.
- Oddball: hold in the sword room corner with the sword bot; others on the lift top and walkway.

### Zanzibar (One-flag CTF, One-bomb)

- Callouts: Base (the fortress with the flag), Base roof, Wheel (the windmill with rockets), Camp Froman (the sniper nest on the beach side), Beach, Gate (the main gate, opened by the switch), Seawall, Warthog spawn, Ghost spawn.
- Attack: the hog drives the beach with a gunner; one bot takes the sniper at Camp Froman; two push the gate switch and the wheel for rockets; the runner enters by the seawall ladder.
- Defense: sniper on the base roof covering the beach; shotgun inside the base at the flag; one at the gate controls; one on the wheel with rockets covering the hog lane.
- Grenade spots: onto the base roof from the wheel; into the flag room from the upper walkway.

### Coagulation (8v8 CTF)

- Callouts: Red and Blue base (roof, inside, back), Caves (overshield side and camo side), Mid rocks, Cliffs, the Teleporter.
- Setup: snipers on base roofs; the tank with an infantry escort on the home side; Banshee hunts the enemy hog; two hogs with gunners; the flag runner rides the hog passenger seat around the cliff edge. Rockets and plasma from the caves counter vehicles.

### Turf, Foundation, Elongation, Headlong, Relic, Containment

- Turf: rooftops and the Wraith; hold the rooftops with BRs and shotguns on the stairs; the Warthog is a trap in the narrow streets.
- Foundation: a four-way symmetrical interior; hold two adjacent corners and the ramps; shotguns on the stairs.
- Elongation: conveyor belts carry grenades; hold the upper walkways and nade the belts; the sword at the center.
- Headlong: the crane and the bridge building are the sniper spots; the hog runs the main street and the Banshee hunts it.
- Relic: the big tower controls the beach; attackers use the hog and the teleporter.
- Containment: Wraiths and tanks; hold the base rooftops with snipers and keep the tank behind the gate.

## Halo 3 playbooks

Timers are the MCC default or MLG values from the main tab's section 7; callouts are the MLG-era names.

### The Pit (Slayer, CTF, KOTH)

- Callouts: Red and Blue base (Flag spawn inside), Sniper tower (Snipe 1 bottom, Snipe 2 top, per side), Sword room (center, the small building), Sword bridge (the walkway over it), Rockets (the center tunnel under the mid), Shotgun hall (each team's side hall), Needle hall (opposite side hall), Training yard (the open center with pillars), Green side (overshield or custom powerup), Long hall (the back corridor), Camo (the opposite powerup), Shortcut (between flag and sniper tower).
- Opening: two throw grenades into the rocket tunnel from the yard and push rockets; the sniper goes to its tower; the fourth holds the shotgun hall corner. If the enemy gets rockets, the team falls back to the sniper tower and base and waits for the two shots to be spent.
- Standard hold (Slayer): sniper at Snipe 2 covering the yard and the enemy sniper tower; one BR at Sword room holding the center; one at the home shotgun hall; one roaming between the needle hall and Long hall for grenades and flanks.
- CTF: three captures win in MLG. The runner pulls the flag and goes Shortcut to Sword room side or through the needle hall; the sniper covers from the home tower; rockets escort. Defense: a sniper in the tower with a regenerator, a shotgun in the base, a BR at the Sword bridge ([Prima Pit guide](https://primagames.com/eguides/halo-3-odst-eguide/multiplayer/multiplayer-maps/the-pit)).
- Grenade spots: into the rocket tunnel from both ends; onto Snipe 2 from the yard; over the Sword room walls into the needle hall; into the shotgun hall from the yard.
- Jumps: crouch-jump from the yard pillars onto the Sword bridge; from the shotgun hall onto the base wall; onto the Sword room roof with a gravity lift.
- KOTH: hills cycle around the yard and halls; hold the Sword room and the tower nearest the hill.

### Guardian (Slayer, Oddball)

- Callouts: Sniper tower (Snipe 1 bottom, Snipe 2 middle, Snipe 3 top mid), Gold (the gold lift and its top), Blue room (the blue-lit room with the shotgun), Green (the green corridor and lift), Elbow (the bend near Snipe 1), Hammer (the bottom center), Mauler (near Blue room), Camo (the camo spawn at bottom), Top mid (Snipe 3's platform), the Jumps (the rocks between Gold and Snipe 3).
- Opening: two push Snipe 2 and Snipe 3 for the sniper; one goes Gold for the lift top; one goes Blue room for the shotgun and holds its doorway. The hammer at the bottom is grabbed only when the top is held.
- Standard hold: Top mid (Snipe 3), Gold top, Blue room, with Snipe 2 as the fallback. From these the team sees Green lift, the jumps, Elbow and Hammer.
- Oddball: hold the ball in Blue room's back corner or at Snipe 1 behind the pillar; teammates at Snipe 2, Top mid and Gold. Grenades at the Blue room door are the main defense.
- Grenade spots: onto Top mid from Gold; into Blue room from Green; onto the Gold lift landing from Top mid; down to Hammer from Snipe 2.
- Jumps: the Gold-to-Top mid rock jump; the Snipe 1 to Snipe 2 ledge; Blue room ledge to Gold.
- Flow rule: never climb into a held room; drop down, rotate, and come up elsewhere.

### Construct (Slayer, KOTH)

- Callouts: Top mid (sniper tower), Gold lift and Gold top, Purple lift and Purple top, Street (the lower level long corridor), Bottom mid (sword), Laser (the laser spawn on the lower side), Camo (top, in MLG after 2:00), the Windows, Closed (the closed-off side room).
- Opening: three rush the lifts to Top mid, Gold and Purple; one takes the sword at Bottom mid or the laser and returns up.
- Standard hold: all four on top (Top mid, Gold, Purple, and a floater). Fire down into Street through the windows; the laser from top one-shots anyone in Street. The lifts are the only way up and are pre-aimed.
- KOTH: hills include top and Street positions; the team holds top and sends one down to contest Street hills while the others shoot down.
- Grenade spots: onto the lift landings from top; into Street from the windows; onto Top mid from Gold.

### Narrows (Slayer, CTF)

- Callouts: Red and Blue base (Base top with the sniper, Flag inside), Top mid (the bridge top), Bottom mid (under the bridge, rockets), the Man cannons (each base to the far side), the Landings (where the man cannons land), Overshield and Camo (at the landings or mid per variant), the Ramps, the Pit.
- Opening: sniper to the home base top; two to Bottom mid for rockets with grenades first; one at Top mid.
- Standard hold: base top (sniper) and Top mid with two BRs; the fourth floats between Bottom mid and the landings. The man cannons are used only to chase a flag runner or to flank when the enemy is weak.
- CTF: three captures win in MLG. The runner goes Bottom mid to the home side; escorts at Top mid; the sniper covers the enemy's landing. Defense: a sniper on base top, a BR at the ramp, a shotgun in the base.
- Grenade spots: onto the landings timed to a man cannon rider; into Bottom mid from Top mid; onto the enemy base top from Top mid.

### Heretic (Midship remake: Slayer, CTF)

- Same callouts as Midship; the sword and shotgun are the power weapons and the BR/carbine spawn on short timers (15 s per the MCC list).
- The Halo 3 version plays slower: hold Top mid and Pink 3 with BRs, team shoot across, and keep the sword at the lift tops.

### Valhalla (BTB CTF, Slayer)

- Callouts: Red and Blue base (Base top, Inside, Base roof), Laser rock (the center boulder with the laser), the Hills (each side), River (the creek down the middle), Caves (the side tunnels), Pelican or Hornet pad per variant, Missile pod, the Turrets.
- Setup: one bot takes the laser from Laser rock and holds a hillside with cover; the hog with a gunner runs the river edge; the sniper on the base top; the Mongoose carries the flag runner. Anyone on foot in the river is dead to the hog.
- Anti-vehicle: the laser from the hills; the missile pod from the base roof; plasma pistol from the caves for a free hijack.
- CTF: the Mongoose or hog passenger carries; the route is the cave side, not the river.

### High Ground (One-flag CTF, One-bomb)

- Callouts: Base (the fortress, Flag at the top), Bunker (the sniper nest on the beach side of the base wall), Gate (opened by the switch inside), Pipe (the tunnel under the base), Beach (rockets), Rocks, Turret, the Switch room.
- Attack: rockets from the beach; the laser if enabled; one bot opens the gate while the hog waits; the runner uses the pipe. The sniper at the beach rocks counters the bunker.
- Defense: a sniper in the bunker; a shotgun at the pipe exit; a BR at the switch room; the laser on the base roof.

### Last Resort (One-flag, One-bomb)

- Same as Zanzibar with the laser in the base; the laser from the base roof denies the hog, so attackers push the sniper at Camp Froman first to kill the laser user.

### Standoff (BTB Slayer, CTF)

- Callouts: Red and Blue base (bunkers), Mid rocks (laser and rockets), Side bunkers, the Hogs.
- Setup: two hogs with gunners circle the outer edges; the laser bot sits in the base bunker; snipers on the bunker tops. The laser decides the hog war; the bot without it hides behind the rocks.

### Snowbound, Sandtrap, Blackout, Citadel, Cold Storage, Ghost Town, Orbital, Rat's Nest, Avalanche, Foundry

- Snowbound: the caves with shotgun; the laser denies the Ghosts; the lift top overlooks the center.
- Sandtrap: board the Elephant as a team; the laser from the temple top; the Chopper rams everything.
- Blackout: Lockout's callouts (S1 to S3, BR tower, Library, Elbow, Bottom mid) and setups apply with Halo 3 weapons; the sword in Library, shotgun at Bottom mid.
- Citadel: a tower-and-ring map; hold the top ring and the rockets at the bottom on timer.
- Cold Storage: Chill Out's callouts; the rocket ledge and the teleporter.
- Ghost Town: the sniper on the upper walkway; rockets in the center building; the shotgun in the tunnels.
- Orbital: the long corridor; the sniper at one end, the rockets mid, the mauler at the side rooms.
- Rat's Nest and Avalanche: vehicle loops; hogs with gunners and the laser from the interior.
- Foundry (Amplified, Onslaught MLG variants): symmetrical forge maps; hold the elevated center and nade the ramps; timers per MLG.

## Halo: Reach playbooks

MLG Reach timers: rockets 178 s, sniper and grenade launcher 118 s, powerups 120 s. Default playlist layouts differ; the scripting agent reads the variant.

### Countdown (Slayer, CTF, KOTH)

- Callouts: Red and Blue side (each with a Lift, a Sniper ledge, a Shotgun hall), Top mid (the bridge over the center), Bottom mid (the center lift room with the sword), the Lifts (to Top mid from each base), the Windows (the side overlooks), Grenade launcher (center per variant).
- Opening: two push Top mid; one takes the home sniper ledge; one times the sword at Bottom mid.
- Standard hold: Top mid plus the home sniper ledge; the sword holder at the bottom of the enemy's lift. Jetpackers crossing the center are needle rifle targets.
- CTF: flags at the base floors; the runner goes Bottom mid and up the home lift; escorts on Top mid. The sword holder guards the home lift bottom.
- Grenade spots: into the lift rooms from Top mid; onto Top mid from the windows.

### Zealot (Slayer, CTF)

- Callouts: Red and Blue room (the two team rooms), Top mid, Bottom mid (sword), Purple (the lower corridor), the Space deck (the low-gravity upper level, often blocked in MLG), Grenade launcher, Concussion rifle.
- Setup: hold the top ring: both team rooms and Top mid. The sword holder watches the Purple ramp. In objective games the space deck is off limits.
- CTF: the runner goes Bottom mid to Purple to the home room; escorts on the ring.

### Reflection (Ivory Tower remake: Slayer, Oddball)

- Callouts and setups as Ivory Tower: Top walkway (rockets), Lift and Lift top, Balcony (sniper), Glass room, Sword room (bottom), Shotgun hall. Jetpack lets a bot skip the lift to the top walkway; the sword holder at the lift bottom punishes lift riders.

### Sword Base (Slayer, KOTH)

- Callouts: Top (the upper floor with the sniper ledge), Long hall (the main floor corridor), Lifts (two), Catwalk (the walkway over the long hall), Sword (top), Shotgun (bottom), Yellow and Green (the two ends by light color), the Office (the side room).
- Setup: hold Top and the catwalk; the lifts are pre-aimed; the shotgun holds the bottom corner only when the team is far ahead. Jetpack from the catwalk to Top is the main shortcut.

### Asylum (Sanctuary remake: CTF, Slayer)

- Callouts and setups as Sanctuary (Snipe, Ring 1 to 3, Rocks, Carbine, Water). MLG Sanctuary is the smoother version; the jetpack lets a flag runner cut across the center ring, which the sniper at Snipe should expect.

### Pinnacle (Ascension remake: Slayer, KOTH)

- Callouts and setups as Ascension: Big tower, Small tower, Bridge, Dish, Back lift, Rocks. The sniper on the Big tower; rockets at the dish; jetpack to the tower top is the shortcut.

### Powerhouse (Slayer, Oddball)

- Callouts: Powerhouse (the main building with the roof), Dam (the raised side), Blue base (the lower rooftop), Creek (the lower water area), Rockets (bottom mid), Sniper (the dam side), Shotgun (inside the powerhouse).
- Setup: hold the powerhouse roof and the dam; the creek is a pit. Oddball: hold inside the powerhouse corner.

### Boardwalk (Slayer, KOTH)

- Callouts: Overlook (the balcony with the sniper), Street (the long open lane), Arcade (the interiors), Stairs, Concussion.
- Setup: hold the overlook and the arcade doors; the street is crossed only crouched behind the planters.

### Hemorrhage (Blood Gulch remake: BTB CTF)

- Callouts and setups as Blood Gulch: base roofs with snipers; caves with the laser and plasma launcher; hogs with gunners around the cliff edge; the Revenant for fast flag taxi; the Scorpion with an escort; the Wraith only with cover.

### Spire, Paradiso, Breakpoint, Tempest, Highlands, The Cage, Uncaged

- Spire: the Spire top is the objective; Falcons ferry attackers; the laser at the base denies them.
- Paradiso: the center hill overlooks everything; hogs run the perimeter; the plasma launcher from the hill.
- Breakpoint: Invasion phases; attackers use the Falcon and Warthog, defenders the Wraith and the interior.
- Tempest: two bases across a beach; the laser at mid; the hog on the beach.
- Highlands: a wide valley; snipers on the hills; the hog on the road.
- The Cage and Uncaged: forge maps with a central tower; hold the tower top and nade the ramps.

## Halo 4 playbooks

Fixed power weapons vary by variant; most arrive through ordnance. The playbooks name the ordnance spots and the positions that see them.

### Haven (Slayer, CTF, KOTH, Extraction)

- Callouts: Top mid (the center ring), Bottom mid (under it), Red and Blue base (with the flag and a lift each), the Lifts (two per side to the ring), Side rooms (the curved side corridors), Top ordnance (the mid drop), Bottom ordnance (the side drops), the Rails.
- Opening: three to Top mid via the lifts with grenades thrown over the ring; one holds the home base.
- Standard hold: Top mid plus the home lift exit; shoot down into the side rooms. Thruster off the ring to reset.
- CTF: the runner takes the flag and goes Bottom mid to the home lift; the ring holders shoot down; the carrier's magnum is used at Short band if caught.
- Ordnance: the Top mid drop is contested by two with pulse grenades thrown onto it.

### Adrift (Slayer, CTF)

- Callouts: Center hall (the long middle), Outer walkways (the two sides), the Lifts, Red and Blue base, Center ordnance.
- Setup: hold the home side of the center hall and the adjacent outer walkway; DMRs down the walkways; the center ordnance is contested from the home side.

### Abandon (Slayer, Oddball)

- Callouts: Tower (the center tower top), Ramps (the ramps to it), Jungle paths (the lower routes), Sniper (top of the tower per variant), the Rocks.
- Setup: hold the tower top and the ramp heads; the jungle paths are for flankers only. Oddball: hold at the tower base corner with the tower top covering.

### Solace (Slayer, CTF)

- Callouts: Top mid (the center bridge), Side ledges, Base balconies, the Lifts, Mid ordnance (rockets or binary).
- Setup: hold Top mid and the home balcony; team shoot across; the mid ordnance is contested with two.

### Complex (Slayer, CTF)

- Callouts: Roof (the main building roof), Long hall, Courtyard, the Garage, the Stairs, Sniper (roof per variant).
- Setup: hold the roof and the long hall; the courtyard is crossed only during enemy reloads.

### Ragnarok (Valhalla remake: BTB CTF, Slayer)

- Callouts and setups as Valhalla; the Mantis replaces the hog as the main threat: EMP it with a plasma pistol, then board; the laser from Laser rock; the Warthog runs the river edge; the Banshee hunts the Mantis from behind.
